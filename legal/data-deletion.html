Delete My Data

We respect your right to control your personal information.

You can request deletion of all or part of the personal data we hold about you (also known as the right to be forgotten or right to erasure).

Important:
Deleting your data is permanent and irreversible.
After processing your request:
  • You will no longer be able to log in or use the service
  • All your content, messages, settings, history, etc. will be permanently removed
  • Recovery will not be possible — even if you contact support later

How to request data deletion

Please send your deletion request from the same email address you used to register / sign in with us.

Send an email containing the following information:

Subject: DATA DELETION REQUEST – 

I request the complete deletion of my personal data pursuant to
Article 17 GDPR / CCPA / applicable privacy law.

Account email:       
Username (if any):   
Full name (optional): 
Approximate sign-up date (optional): 

I understand that this action is irreversible.

Signed,
Your name

Send the email to:

privacy-delete@yourdomain.com

We will normally confirm receipt within 72 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days (as required by most privacy regulations).

We may ask you to verify your identity before processing the request. This is to protect your data from unauthorized deletion requests.

Exceptions – data we may keep

Some data may need to be retained for legal reasons even after a deletion request, for example:

  • Financial / tax / invoicing records (usually 6–10 years)
  • Proof of fulfilled deletion requests
  • Abuse / fraud / legal violation logs
  • Data that has been irreversibly anonymized

These exceptions are very limited and we only keep what the law strictly requires.
