Data Deletion Request

Last updated: January 2026

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data we hold about you (subject to certain legal exceptions such as financial record-keeping obligations, fraud prevention, or legal claims).

Important: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible.
Once processed, you will permanently lose access to your account, content, matches, messages, progress, and all associated data.

How to request deletion

The fastest way is to use the form below.

Request received ✓

We have received your deletion request.
You will receive a confirmation email within a few minutes.

Processing usually takes 1–15 days depending on verification.

What happens after you submit?

  1. You’ll receive a confirmation email (check spam folder)
  2. We may ask you to verify identity (usually via reply to that email)
  3. After verification, your account enters a deletion queue
  4. Data is permanently removed from active systems within 30 days
  5. Some anonymized / aggregated / legally required data may be retained

Alternative deletion methods

  • Inside the app: Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete Account
  • Email: privacy@yourdomain.com
